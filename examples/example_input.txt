Heavy rainfall in mid-July caused the river Ahr to overflow its banks within a few hours. In Altenahr, the B267 federal road was severely damaged over a length of two kilometres and had to be closed. Around 3,000 households in the district were without electricity after parts of the electricity grid near Bad Neuenahr were flooded, which also stopped the pumping stations of the drinking water supply.

The railway line between Remagen and Ahrbrück was destroyed at several points. The operator estimated the repair costs at about €1.3 billion. Thousands of commuters had to use replacement buses for months.

The sewage treatment plant in Sinzig was inundated, and untreated wastewater flowed into the river. Debris and waste were deposited on around 100 hectares of farmland along the river banks.
